Introduction

  2. 1.2Approximate unlearning by post-hoc weight editingThe deployment case for a method that touches weights once, needs no optimiser state and no retain-set training loop.
  3. 1.3The predecessor result: an exhaustive search that found nothingevidence in hand10,534 instance-level strategies, 3 operator families, 5 selectors, 4 objective formulations. CH 7

Analysis and Discussion

Where the dissertation is won. The results are mixed; the analysis is what turns mixed results into a thesis.

2,600 w
  1. 7.1RQ1 — structure is a precondition, not a correlateevidence in handThe falsifiable prediction from Chapter 1 survived its test. Say what would have refuted it.
  2. 7.2RQ3 — what predicts per-class successneeds an experimentRegress per-class ACC_f on the activation-contrast statistic and on class confusability. If the ten points fall on a line, the method's failure mode becomes a property of the data that can be predicted before any unlearning is attempted. This is the strongest contribution available to you.
  3. 7.3The truck counterexampleevidence in hand42.10 pure, 30.90 hybrid, against a 7.55 mean — the largest absolute gain of any class and still the worst result. Refinement narrows the gap; it does not close it. Give it its own section and let it stand.
  4. 7.4The membership-inference discrepancyAnchor MIA reads 92–95% where your own AUC sits near 0.55 on the same models. Retraining scoring exactly 100.00 is evidence the anchor metric is saturated, not a target to hit. A genuine methodological observation that costs only writing.
  5. 7.5The forgetting–retention frontierWhat the front's shape says about the trade-off, and where C* sits on it — including whether a different selection rule would serve a deployer better than the composite.
  6. 7.6Positioning: what MED-US buys and what it costsArgue the axis you win on — no gradients, no retain-set loop, an explicit Pareto front instead of a single operating point — while conceding ACC_f plainly.
  7. 7.7Threats to validityInternal: selection protocol, a single W_0. External: one dataset, one architecture. Construct: what MIA certifies. Conclusion: ten classes, few seeds.
  8. 7.8LimitationsDistinct from 7.7 — what the method cannot do, rather than what the study might have got wrong.
